Questions

    
 

    
Do you need my password?
No. We only need a public link or username. Never share your password with anyone.

    
Should my account be public?
Yes. Private accounts can't receive the service, so switch to public until the order completes.

    
How long does an order take?
Most orders start soon after payment is verified. Larger quantities take longer to deliver.

    
Can the numbers drop later?
Some drop is normal on social platforms. Platforms can also remove accounts or activity, so results are not guaranteed.

    
I paid but nothing started.
